Pit Crew Arena · prototype

Drive the car. Let the crew build it.

  1. Opening garage (20 s): choose Speed, Armor or Close-range.
  2. Battle (60 s): you steer with WASD or the arrow keys. Weapons fire on their own triggers, and SPACE fires any part wired to “driver”.
  3. Pit stop (15 s): your crew chief reads the telemetry and proposes two legal upgrades with different trade-offs. Pick one, give an instruction, or keep your build.
  4. Reveal: all four crews show their changes at once. Then the next round starts.

Scoring: 3 per wreck, 1 per 25 damage dealt, and 2 for surviving, but only if you dealt at least 20 damage. Cars are repaired every round. Each pit stop pays 5 credits plus up to 3 salvage.

How the crew works

At round end your crew gets the blueprint, your credits, the telemetry (collisions, time at each range, damage by side and source, launcher accuracy, empty-battery moments), what the rivals visibly run, your instruction, and the upgrade history. It sends back one observation and two modifications.

Every proposal goes through the validator: mounts, triggers, power slots, weight, credits and your locked part. If the validator rejects one, the crew gets the exact error and one retry. After that, or if the crew misses the deadline, a scripted backup crew fills in. The previous legal build always stands if nothing is chosen.

The crew picks parts, mounts and triggers. It can't set damage values, change rules or touch other cars.
